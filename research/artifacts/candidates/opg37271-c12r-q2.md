## Lower-bound argument and realizability

At a node with stem color a and child states (b_i,L_i), a transition is admissible precisely when all stem colors are distinct, every L_i(a)<=2, and, for two children, L_1(b_2)+L_2(b_1)<=3. The outgoing b_i length is 1+L_i(a); other lengths are one. This accounts for every path crossing the common vertex. The graph is a tree, so there are no cycle obstructions.

The cost is the sum of child costs plus [a differs from the old stem color], except that the new root stem uv is uncharged. Minimize over ALL admissible target tuples and cap at four. C08's induction proves exactness; C09's nonnegative capping identities preserve every comparison with the edit threshold.

Thus this is a genuinely reachable derivation from seed branches, not an abstract profile satisfying only coordinate ranges.
