# C15L prior-art comparison

Verdict: candidate_only. Retrieved 2026-09-06.

Hui Lei, Yongtang Shi and Zi-Xia Song, *Star chromatic index of subcubic multigraphs*, arXiv:1701.04105v3, 22 November 2017, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1701.04105 .

Lemma 3.2(a,b) and Corollary 3.3(a) contain the corresponding restrictions for star-critical multigraphs. C15L treats simple graphs, explicitly reconstructs the extension maps and audits all sufficient palette choices; it is not a novelty claim. Its degree-three lemma permits absent arms but requires the neighbors to be pairwise nonadjacent. Minimal-counterexample use separately excludes neighbor adjacencies via the triangle lemma. The source's additional restrictions in Lemma 3.2(c,d) are not silently imported.
