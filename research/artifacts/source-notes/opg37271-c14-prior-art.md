# C14B source comparison

Verdict: candidate_only. Retrieved 2026-09-06.

Primary source: Zdenek Dvorak, Bojan Mohar, Robert Samal, *Star chromatic index*, arXiv:1011.3376v2 (15 December 2011), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1011.3376 .

Theorem 5.1(a) states the seven-color upper bound. Lemma 5.2(a) is a sufficient extension test using a color absent from edges at line-graph distance at most two. In the proof of Theorem 5.1, the cut-edge reduction reserves a seventh color against at most six nearby edges. This does not supply a six-color spare color. C14B instead colors augmented shores with pendant stubs and aligns their palettes; its vertex-minimal consequence excludes only non-pendant bridges. No full six-color theorem is inferred from this source.

The PDF's printed page 7 (zero-based page 6) was read by screenshot as well as parsed text. The definitions and theorem labels agree. This note records attribution and scope, not proof verification or novelty.
