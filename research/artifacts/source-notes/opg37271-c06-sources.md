## C. Prior leaf-neighborhood analysis

Hui Lei, Yongtang Shi and Zi-Xia Song, Star chromatic index of subcubic multigraphs.
Locator: https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.04105
Read PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1701.04105
The PDF header identifies arXiv:1701.04105v3, 22 November 2017. Read: Section 3, Lemma 3.1(a)-(c) and their proofs, printed pages 6-7. A fresh PDF screenshot request failed; this comparison uses parsed text.

The lemma assumes a globally star k-critical subcubic multigraph, k=5 or 6. For k=6 its leaf-neighbor conclusions overlap C01's saturation structure. Translate their leaf x to our v, their y to our u, and their y1,y2 to our x,y. Their proof uses nonexistence of a full coloring to force local conditions. C01 instead assumes nonextendibility of one fixed coloring in the simple-graph setting. The overlap requires attribution; it does not make the universal fixed-color extension claim true.
