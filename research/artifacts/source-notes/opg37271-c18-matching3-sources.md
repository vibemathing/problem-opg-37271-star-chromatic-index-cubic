Primary source: Carl Johan Casselgren, Jonas B. Granholm, Andre Raspaud, *On star edge colorings of bipartite and subcubic graphs*, arXiv:1912.02467v2 (27 January 2020), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.02467 .

Lemma 2.9, printed page 6, excludes C5 from its three-color cycle list theorem. Proposition 3.2, printed pages 11-12, proves a six-color bound for subcubic planar graphs of girth at least seven with a perfect matching. Its proof contracts the matching, three-colors the resulting triangle-free planar graph, and combines that matching coloring with a disjoint three-color complement coloring.

C18M retains the actual matching-conflict three-colorability as a premise, but does not assume planarity or girth. Its patch for five-cycle components explicitly addresses a case excluded by the cited girth hypothesis.
